01 / THE WORKFLOW

Everything you need.
Nothing in the way.

Designed to make the next useful piece of information easy to find, without crowding the calculation.

01

Weather, interpreted clearly

Enter conditions manually or use the app's weather input tools. Snowfall intensity is derived from relevant visibility and day/night conditions when applicable.

02

Fluid-specific selection

Choose the fluid and applicable dilution. Temperature and fluid limitations stay connected to the result.

03

Restrictions that stand out

Valid states remain quiet. Prohibited conditions are explained next to their cause, with no ambiguous green result to distract from the warning.

04

A session that stays legible

Follow remaining holdover time, view the start and expiry in local time or UTC, and update weather during the session.
